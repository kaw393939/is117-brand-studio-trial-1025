    Why this direction looks this way

    A / Modernist exploration

    Original web composition informed by a viewed design reference. No museum artwork is reproduced. Audience effects below are intentions, not research findings.

    01 / A disciplined grid

    Josef Müller-Brockmann, Musica Viva, 1957 · MoMA. Four compact text columns at the top become a four-part header. The aligned content columns aim to make the invitation easy to read; the risk is a tone that feels too formal.

    02 / Space, then rhythm

    The reference’s open center and lengthening diagonal black rules become generous breathing room and a short sequence of original rules below the shirt. The intended pace is unhurried; the risk is that extra space pushes product details farther down a small screen.

    03 / Recognition without a loud logo

    A consistent green frame, restrained type hierarchy, and repeated alignment carry the identity around the unchanged white shirt. This aims to feel recognizable yet everyday. The risk is a quiet identity that the intended café audience may find too anonymous.
